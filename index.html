Earn money with passenger rides, restaurant food deliveries, and parcel runs. Park, select neutral or hold the handbrake, then press T to open your phone and click an order. Using your phone while moving starts a police chase. Close it before driving. Parcel pickups are in servo forecourts, reached via the marked driveways. High-speed crashes can kill you; smaller impacts damage your car. Repeated crashes also end your run. Follow the yellow pickup beacon, stop for 2.5 seconds, then take your passenger or delivery to the green beacon. Drive safely for a bonus.

55 L tank (start with 38 L): Fuel burns while driving and idling. Fuel up at petrol stations (green dots): stop at a pump, press F to fuel, then P to tap your card ($2.10/L, you start with $800). Drive off without paying and the police come. Safe houses (blue dots): drive in and stop for 4 s to lose the cops and save a checkpoint. Start in neutral: hold clutch, press E for 1st, give throttle, ease off the clutch.

First gear reaches about 30 km/h at 6,000 rpm; shift deliberately with the clutch. Explore the winding Eastern Scenic Drive, its two overpasses (cross underneath on the city streets), huge roundabouts at George St / 800 m north and the southwest district, Scenic Hills and the outer M1 highway (100 km/h). Follow ONE WAY signs. Give way at zebra crossings: hitting a pedestrian ends your run. Orange F on the map marks fast-food pickups; P marks police stations. Out of fuel? Stop and press H for 8 L roadside delivery ($65).
