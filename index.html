Earn money with passenger rides, restaurant food deliveries, and parcel runs. Park, select neutral or hold the handbrake, then press T to open your phone and click an order. Using your phone while moving starts a police chase. Close it before driving. Parcel pickups are in servo forecourts, reached via the marked driveways. Ram police vehicles at speed to damage and destroy them; roadblock cars can also be destroyed. Follow the yellow pickup beacon, stop for 2.5 seconds, then take your passenger or delivery to the green beacon. Drive safely for a bonus.

150 L tank: Fuel up at petrol stations (green dots): stop at a pump, press F to fuel, then P to tap your card ($2.10/L, you start with $800). Drive off without paying and the police come. Safe houses (blue dots): drive in and stop for 4 s to lose the cops and save a checkpoint. Start in neutral: hold clutch, press E for 1st, give throttle, ease off the clutch.
